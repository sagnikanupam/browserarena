Day 1: Arrival & Gentle Introduction

Day 2: Classic Temples & Zen Gardens
• Morning: Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) at opening (8:30 am) to avoid crowds. Walk its reflective pond garden.  
• Midday: Head to Ryoan-ji to see its world-famous rock garden. Sit in quiet contemplation.  
• Afternoon: Continue to Ninna-ji, explore its temple complex and maple-lined path.  
• Evening: Relax in your hotel’s onsen or public bath (if available), followed by kaiseki-style dinner.  

Day 3: Southern Higashiyama & Spiritual Ascent
• Morning: Early-rise hike up the winding torii at Fushimi Inari-Taisha. Enjoy sunrise light through the gates.  
• Midday: Lunch at a street-stall alley near Inari—try inarizushi and local snacks.  
• Afternoon: Head north to Sanjusangendo Hall. Admire the rows of 1,001 Kannon statues.  
• Evening: Leisurely walk along the Philosopher’s Path, stopping at quaint cafés or small shrines for reflection.  

Day 4: Tea Ceremony & Gion Culture
• Morning: Participate in a traditional matcha tea ceremony at a Machiya-style tea house in Higashiyama. Learn the rituals and taste ceremony sweets.  
• Midday: Visit Kennin-ji, Kyoto’s oldest Zen temple, and explore its gardens.  
• Afternoon: Explore Gion’s historic streets—pop into artisan shops (textiles, incense, pottery).  
• Evening: Attend a small geisha-dance performance (dinner optional), or book a private kaiseki/private maiko experience.  

Day 5: Day Trip to Nara
• Morning: Take the JR Nara line (45 min). Arrive at Nara Park → feed bowing deer.  
• Midday: Visit Todai-ji’s Daibutsu (Great Buddha). Explore Nigatsu-do for city views.  
• Afternoon: Walk to Kasuga Taisha, enjoy lantern-lit paths. Sit by Kasuga Primeval Forest for a quiet pause.  
• Evening: Return to Kyoto. Light dinner near the station and early bed or a soak at your ryokan.  

Day 6: Arashiyama Bamboo & Riverside Calm
• Morning: Early Arashiyama bamboo grove walk—beat the crowds.  
• Midday: Visit Tenryu-ji temple and its moss and bamboo garden; lunch riverside (soba or tofu specialties).  
• Afternoon: Rent a boat or pedal-boat on the Hozu River for a mellow perspective. Then stroll the Ōi riverside embankment.  
• Evening: Dine at a riverside restaurant in Arashiyama. Return to Kyoto center by train.  

Day 7: Modern Meets Ancient & Leisurely Farewell
• Morning: Explore Kyoto Imperial Palace Park or the Kyoto National Museum for art and history.

• Afternoon: Free time for shopping in Nishiki Market or craft souvenir hunting in downtown.  ￼

– Dress in light layers; mornings/evenings can be cool.

– Purchase a prepaid IC card (ICOCA/Suica) for easy subway and bus travel.  
– Use weekdays/early mornings for popular sites (Inari, Arashiyama, Kinkaku-ji).
